SYNTHETIC MOCK DOCUMENT
This document contains fictional, synthetically created data. It is
not intended for clinical care, coding, billing, legal interpretation, or payer
submission.

1. PURPOSE
This synthetic guide identifies situations in which an automated workflow should
route output to a qualified human reviewer instead of making a definitive claim.

2. ESCALATE FOR MISSING SUPPORT
Escalate when available documents do not establish the requested diagnosis,
status, complication, linkage, severity, or laterality. A low-confidence response
or insufficient-evidence result is safer than a plausible unsupported answer.

3. ESCALATE FOR CONFLICT
Escalate when two source documents conflict about active status, diagnosis,
patient association, or relevant chronology. The workflow may summarize the
conflict with citations, but it must not silently decide which source is correct.

4. ESCALATE FOR CONSEQUENTIAL USE
Qualified clinical or coding review is required before any generated output can
affect a patient chart, diagnosis list, billing action, risk-adjustment filing,
or payer submission. A source-grounded system may assist review; it is not an
autonomous decision maker.

5. RECORD THE LIMITATION
The response should preserve a review status, missing-information statement, and
source citations. Operational logs should record failures such as unavailable
tools or invalid structured output without exposing real patient information.
